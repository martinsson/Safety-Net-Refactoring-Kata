# Removal and resettlement allowance (RRA)

## Purpose

When the regional administration of Halvermoor moves one of its agents to another outpost, the
agent receives a removal and resettlement allowance. It helps with the cost of the move itself
(distance) and of settling a household in a new place. The posting office keys in a **transfer
file**; the allowance desk checks it, computes the amount, decides how it is paid and issues a
**notice** to the agent. The desk also keeps a ledger of the files it has settled.

## Vocabulary

- **Agent**: a civil servant of the administration, identified by an agent id. Agents belong to a
  **category**: A, B and C for established staff, D for contract staff.
- **Outpost**: a place of work. Outposts are identified by a three-letter code (BRF Brennick Ford,
  OLM Oldmere, OLX Oldmere Annex, PAL Port Aldry, CVR Castel Varro, SWH Saltwick Halt, FNR Fennick
  Reach, DMR Dunmarrow, IQL Isle of Quell, VSL Varro Sluice, HGP Harrow Gap). All of them lie on the
  Trunk Road, at a known kilometre marker, except the Isle of Quell, reached by the ferry. Harrow
  Gap, a mountain outpost, opened in June 2015.
- **Remote outpost**: an outpost with a coefficient above 1 (Fennick Reach, Dunmarrow, Isle of
  Quell, Harrow Gap). Life there is harder and the allowance is increased.
- **Transfer in the interest of the service**: the administration decided the move. **Transfer at
  the agent's own request**: the agent asked for it; it is paid at a reduced rate and contract staff
  are not covered.
- **Household**: the agent, a partner and children moving with the agent.
- **Old regime**: files with a request date before 1 July 2011 follow the rules in force at the
  time.
- **Notice**: the letter sent to the agent: status (granted or rejected), amount, how it is paid,
  and a short text explaining the decision. Each file receives a reference number.

## Main rules

**Admissibility.** A file is rejected when its request date is missing or later than the
processing date, when an outpost code is unknown (or the outpost did not exist yet at the request
date), or when an agent of category D asks for a transfer at their own request. A transfer of less
than 20 km is a move within the same locality and gives no allowance.

**Distance.** The distance is measured along the Trunk Road between the two outposts'
kilometre markers; crossing to or from the Isle of Quell adds the ferry crossing. Saltwick Halt
was moved along the road in 2014, so older files use its former position. Distances fall into
three bands (under 200 km, under 500 km, beyond) with a decreasing rate per kilometre; the rates
of the longest band were revised in 2018.

**Amount.** The starting point is a base amount that depends on the category, increased for each
member of the household (with a limit). A distance supplement is added. Several supplements and
coefficients then apply: a housing shortage supplement for outposts where housing is scarce (since
2019 and 2021), a winter installation supplement for arrivals at Harrow Gap during the winter
months, the coefficient of a remote destination, and a seniority increase. An agent who leaves a
remote outpost for an ordinary one after several years there receives a return bonus.

Transfers at the agent's own request receive a reduced share of the base and of the distance
supplement, no distance supplement at all for short moves, and simpler seniority and remote
outpost rules.

**Successive transfers.** The ledger remembers the last settled file of each agent. A second
transfer within twelve months is paid at half rate. An agent sent back to the outpost they have
just left receives no distance supplement for that return.

**Limits.** The amount is rounded down to a multiple of five, then limited by a ceiling that
depends on the category (old-regime files of category A are not capped). Transfers in the interest
of the service receive at least a minimum amount.

**Payment.** Large families and agents sent to a remote outpost by the service receive part of the
allowance as an advance on notification, provided the request has been with the office for a
month and they have not had a settlement recently. The rest is paid in one payment or in monthly
instalments, depending on the amount and on the household. The notice states the amount, the
advance if any and the instalments.
